The asymmetry

A whole brain can be cheaper to specify than one living cell.

The whole-fly implementation begins after an enormous act of collapse: roughly 50 million anatomical chemical synapses become about 15 million weighted connections, and each neurone becomes two dynamic variables. Applying the same representational generosity to one mammalian neurone immediately outruns any public, simultaneous dataset.

“Cheaper” here means fewer represented state variables, not fewer measurements, less labour or a biologically simpler organism.

The intracellular-light layer does not claim that every organelle is a photoreceptor, or that mitochondrial photobiomodulation has been parameterised in M1 ipRGCs. It marks a category error in the opposite direction: light-responsive intracellular chemistry cannot be assumed absent merely because it lies below a membrane-and-spike model.
