INTRACELLULAR LIGHT RULE

“Light” will not be one slider. Melanopsin’s blue-light stimulus, mitochondrial or redox chromophore absorption, heating and photodamage require separate spectra, compartments and state transitions. A mechanism enters only when its coupling is explicit and falsifiable.
